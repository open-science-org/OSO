What happens next

  1. AI pre-screen. A report for validators. It never rejects on its own.
  2. Validation. 5 randomly drawn validators check admission criteria. Majority admits.
  3. Challenge window. Anyone can challenge for spam or plagiarism. Tokens wait in escrow.
  4. Published. Tokens and your stake are released.
  5. Peer review. 3 reviewers are invited automatically; anyone qualified can add more.

These steps and numbers are this community's setup and can differ in other communities.
